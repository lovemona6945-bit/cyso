Our story

A community built on fellowship.

CYSo (Christian Youth & Student Outing) is a Zimbabwe-based Christian student fellowship founded by Mr. Mugabe. We create welcoming opportunities for students to come together in faith, enjoy meaningful outings and build friendships rooted in Christ.

From our gathering in Harare to the next planned outing at Palawani, Zvishavane, we make space for students from different institutions and churches to pray, worship and encourage one another.
